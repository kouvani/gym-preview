Questions

Do I need a gym?

No. Pick gym, dumbbells at home or no equipment at all, and your plan is built around what you have.

I haven't exercised in years. Is this for me?

Yes. If you're new or coming back after a break, your plan starts gently: a warm-up every session, a few simple moves to learn first, and an easier option whenever you want one.

How long is each workout?

You choose 30, 45 or 60 minutes when you set up the app. That time includes your warm-up.

What if a move doesn't feel right?

Each exercise lists easier options. Move within a range that feels comfortable and pick an easier one whenever you like. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice. If you're unsure whether exercise is right for you, check with a doctor or physiotherapist first.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It opens in your phone's web browser. If you like, you can add it to your home screen and open it like any other app.
